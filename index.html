WELCOME TO SECURE V3

Quick start guide

A short introduction to help you get started. Your available sections depend on your account role.

1. Dashboard

View work, payment and account summaries available to your login.

2. Work Site Submission

Technical Assistants can select an assigned Gram Panchayat, enter work details and measurements, attach a site photo, and submit it for review.

3. Profile Directory & TA Login Access

Administrators manage Technical Assistant profiles and account access. Keep login credentials private.

4. Work Management & Estimate Generator

Administrators record completed works, attach estimate PDFs and generate reports using work-specific measurement fields.

5. Accounts & Payments / Reports

Record payments, review balances and access available reports and audit history.

6. Sign out safely

Your login stays saved on this device until you sign out, clear app data or uninstall the app. Use Sign out on shared devices.

Tap “Quick guide” in the top bar whenever you need these instructions again.
